# Mito

A quest app: it gets people away from their screens by giving them things to do in the real world.

## Language

**Mito**:
The quest app this project is about.

**Quest**:
A task carried out in the real world rather than on a screen.
Spanish: Misión.
_Avoid_: Challenge, mission, task

**Clock**:
The 48 hours a person has to finish a Quest once they accept it.
Spanish: Reloj.
_Avoid_: Timer, deadline

**Voice Note**:
A spoken reflection left on a Quest, heard by the person who left it and by others who get that Quest.
Spanish: Nota de voz.
_Avoid_: Recording, review

**Totem**:
The character a person picks when they start, which levels up as they do.
Spanish: Tótem.
_Avoid_: Avatar, pet

**Stat**:
One of four measures of growth that completed Quests add to: Strength, Intellect, Charisma and Wisdom (Fuerza, Intelecto, Carisma, Sabiduría).
Spanish: Atributo.
_Avoid_: Attribute, score

**Guild**:
A community built around one interest, and the source of a member's daily Quests.
Spanish: Gremio.
_Avoid_: Group, clan, team
